# Book enrichment and reader reception

Research for a decision: "Is this book worth my time, and why might I want to
read it?" Keep researched usefulness distinct from the user's original reason
for saving it.

Prefer primary bibliographic sources for identifiers, edition facts, and
publication data. Use substantive editorial sources for professional assessment
and reader platforms for aggregated reader reception. Record direct source URLs
and a checked/researched timestamp when the KB supports them. Follow configured
source precedence and freshness rules; do not refresh every dynamic value merely
because the record was touched.

## Nonfiction profile

Produce a concise useful summary, normally one to three paragraphs, then cover
central thesis/ideas, intended audience, prerequisites, practical applicability,
likely novelty, evidence/style (for example academic, research-based,
journalistic, anecdotal, or how-to), reasons to read or skip, common praise and
criticism, comparable/overlapping books, accessibility/difficulty, page count,
publication facts, and audiobook duration when available. Check the KB for
meaningful topical overlap without fabricating personalized usefulness.

## Fiction profile

Keep the premise spoiler-free. Cover themes, tone/mood, style, reliably sourced
pacing, intended audience, series and sequence, common praise and criticism,
reasons a reader may or may not enjoy it, comparable books/authors,
accessibility, page count, publication facts, and audiobook duration when
available.

## Reader reception

Keep each platform independent. Goodreads is the preferred broad signal;
StoryGraph is secondary; LibraryThing is secondary/optional, unless the KB
configures different source use. For each available source capture its rating,
rating count, source URL, and research timestamp. Never compute a synthetic
combined score, silently substitute a different platform for missing Goodreads
data, or present an unresolved score as exact.

Synthesize recurring praise and criticism in prose, noting sample-size or
edition uncertainty when material. Keep professional/editorial reviews separate
from reader ratings. If a source is unavailable, blocked, ambiguous, or appears
to describe another work/edition, leave its fields unknown and flag the reason.
